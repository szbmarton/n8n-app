Personal Email Calendar Assistant

Your emails, organized into calendar events.

Personal Email Calendar Assistant is a private productivity application that helps its owner turn relevant email messages into Google Calendar events.

What does it do?

The application connects to the owner's Google account and uses email content to identify event-related information, such as:

  • Event titles and descriptions
  • Dates and times
  • Locations
  • Other relevant scheduling details

Based on the information extracted from email messages, the application creates corresponding events in the owner's Google Calendar.

How it works

  1. Connect Gmail
    The application accesses the owner's Gmail account with explicit authorization.
  2. Analyze emails
    Email content is processed to identify information relevant to calendar scheduling.
  3. Create calendar events
    Relevant information is used to create events in the owner's Google Calendar.

Privacy and security

This is a private, single-user application operated exclusively by its owner. It is not publicly distributed and does not provide public user registration.

Access to Gmail and Google Calendar is granted through Google's OAuth authorization system.

Application data and Google authorization credentials are stored locally on the owner's private home server.

The application uses Google user data only to provide its email-to-calendar functionality.

For more information about data handling, please read our Privacy Policy .
